## **4. 💬 Outreach System (3-Step Sequence)**

You don’t pitch immediately. You build **context → trust → conversion**.

---

### **🔹 Step 1: Connection Request (Short + Natural)**

```
Hi <Name>, noticed you're scaling engineering at <Company>. 
I work with SaaS teams on speeding up releases by eliminating manual QA bottlenecks—would be great to connect.
```

👉 Goal: Acceptance (NOT selling)

---

### **🔹 Step 2: Value Message (After Accept – Day 1)**

```
Thanks for connecting, <Name>.

Quick question—are your teams still relying heavily on manual testing during releases, or is most of it automated now?

I’ve been working with SaaS teams where regression cycles were taking 1–2 days, and we brought that down to a few hours using Playwright + CI.
```

👉 Goal: Start conversation around pain

---

### **🔹 Step 3: Soft Pitch (Only if they respond)**

```
That makes sense—this is exactly where most teams get stuck.

What we typically do is:
- Set up a Playwright-based automation framework
- Cover critical user journeys first
- Integrate it into CI so every release is validated automatically

Happy to walk you through how we approach this—no pitch, just sharing what’s worked.
```

👉 Goal: Get them on a call
