## Critical Rule

DO NOT make the email about Vaibhav.

DO NOT start with:

- I have 15 years experience
    
- I am an expert in...
    
- We specialize in...
    
- We provide QA services...
    

Instead:

FIRST identify the prospect's likely business problem.

Show that you understand their challenge.

Then position Sarathi AI Labs as a possible solution.

---

## Email Structure

### Section 1: Demonstrate Understanding

Analyze the job posting and infer:

- What challenge is this company facing?
    
- Why are they hiring?
    
- What business outcome are they seeking?
    

Examples:

For Playwright roles:

- Legacy Selenium migration
    
- Flaky automation
    
- Faster releases
    

For Banking roles:

- Risk reduction
    
- KYC validation
    
- Regulatory compliance
    

For Startups:

- Faster release cycles
    
- Limited QA bandwidth
    
- Need automation without increasing headcount
    

For Training roles:

- Skill gaps
    
- Upskilling teams
    
- Standardizing automation practices
    

Start the email by discussing that challenge.

---

### Section 2: Connect to Similar Experience

Briefly explain that:

"Through Sarathi AI Labs, we've helped teams facing similar challenges..."

Focus on outcomes.

Examples:

- Reduced regression cycles
    
- Improved release confidence
    
- Accelerated automation adoption
    
- Modernized legacy frameworks
    
- Improved onboarding quality
    
- Reduced production defects
    

---

### Section 3: Position Services

Select only the services relevant to the posting.

Possible offerings:

- Playwright Modernization
    
- AI-Assisted Test Automation
    
- Dedicated QA Teams
    
- Managed Testing Services
    
- Banking QA
    
- API Testing
    
- Enterprise Testing
    
- Test Automation Framework Development
    
- Corporate Training
    
- Quality Engineering Consulting
    

Do NOT dump a long capabilities list.

Mention only services directly relevant to the prospect's challenge.

---

### Section 4: Credibility

Use only relevant credibility.

Examples:

- Banking and Financial Services experience
    
- SaaS and Product Engineering experience
    
- Playwright expertise
    
- Enterprise transformation experience
    
- Corporate training experience
    

Keep this section short.

---

### Section 5: Low Friction CTA

Never ask for a long meeting.

Use:

"If this is a challenge you're currently addressing, I'd be happy to schedule a brief 15-minute conversation to explore whether we can help."
